# Product Design

Anchor decisions in a user trying to accomplish something under real constraints.

- Define the target user, job, trigger, desired outcome, and present obstacle.
- Separate user evidence from assumptions and product goals from vanity metrics.
- Map the primary journey and the most consequential alternative or failure paths.
- Reduce decisions and steps before adding explanation or decoration.
- Decide what information appears now, later, or on demand.
- Specify feedback, recovery, permission, empty, loading, and first-use states.
- Consider accessibility, trust, privacy, abuse, and operational support.
- Define success and guardrail metrics that can falsify the design hypothesis.

Present competing approaches when the tradeoff is real. Recommend a direction,
name its cost, and identify the cheapest prototype or test that reduces uncertainty.
